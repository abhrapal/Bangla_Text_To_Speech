Register a New Speaker

  1. Open the 📖 Phoneme Reading Script in a new tab.
  2. Ask the speaker to read all lines clearly in a quiet environment.
  3. Record a WAV file (16-bit PCM, mono, 22050 Hz or higher).
  4. Target duration: 30–90 seconds (minimum 6 seconds).
  5. Upload the recording: Register Speaker

💡 Tip: Avoid background noise, music, and clipping for best results.
