What Should The Speaker Read?

The speaker should read the complete 📖 Phoneme Reading Script from start to end in a natural speaking style to capture all Bengali phonetic variations.

Recording Requirements

  • Format: WAV file (16-bit PCM, mono)
  • Sample rate: 22050 Hz or 24000 Hz
  • Duration: 30–90 seconds (minimum 6 seconds)
  • Environment: Quiet room, no background noise, fans, or music
  • Quality: Clear speech, steady volume, avoid clipping or distortion
